About the Platform

Building the future of autonomous claims.

VeriClaim is a B2B2C autonomous claims agentic platform that replaces traditional call centers with a multi-agent AI system capable of interviewing, analyzing evidence, detecting fraud, and delivering verdicts — all in real time.

Our Mission

Insurance claims shouldn't take weeks. They shouldn't require 40-minute hold times or endless paperwork. We believe every claim deserves instant, fair, and transparent processing — powered by AI agents that work together like a real team of experts.

The Multi-Agent Architecture

VeriClaim orchestrates 6 specialized AI agents in a hierarchical pipeline. Each agent has a distinct role, tools, and personality.

Voice Orchestrator

Conducts empathetic, human-like interviews. Gathers the full claim story with natural conversation flow.

Policy Triage Agent

Queries the database to verify coverage tier, active status, and risk flags before processing.

Vision Analyst

Performs forensic image analysis using Gemini Vision API. Detects tampering, stock photos, and damage severity.

The Autonomous Pipeline

1

Voice Reception Agent

Engages the claimant in natural conversation, extracting key facts, dates, and incident details dynamically.

2

Vision Analyst

Uses Gemini Vision to inspect uploaded evidence, verifying authenticity and assessing damage severity automatically.

3

Fraud Detective

Cross-references the transcript and vision assessment against policy rules to flag discrepancies or potential fraud.

4

Final Assessor

Synthesizes all agent reports into a final ruling (Approved, Denied, or Escalate) and stores it in the database.

How to Use VeriClaim

1

Start the Voice Agent

Navigate to the Home page and click "Start Conversation". Allow microphone permissions. Speak naturally about your incident, just like you would to a human agent.

2

Provide Evidence

If requested by the agent, click the paperclip icon to upload photos of the damage, invoices, or police reports.

3

Finalize Claim

Once the agent says it has enough information, it will summarize the claim. You can then say "Submit" to send it to the backend assessors.

4

Track Progress

Go to your Dashboard to view the live status of your claim, read the Final Assessor's verdict, and review past history.
