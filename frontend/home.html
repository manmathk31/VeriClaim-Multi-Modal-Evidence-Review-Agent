The Autonomous Claim Flow

Three simple, automated steps to resolve your insurance or service claims with no manual hold times.

Speak & Interview

Engage in an intelligent voice interview. The manager agent asks dynamic questions customized to your specific policy guidelines.

Upload Incident Evidence

Directly drag and drop photos of damage or receipts. The multimodal Vision agent checks details, metadata, and cross-references them instantly.

Instant Final Decision

The policy and fraud agents finalize their review, store the verdict securely in the database, and dispatch a comprehensive email receipt.

Multi-Agent Intelligence

Our system orchestrates 6 distinct AI experts, each dedicated to a portion of the claims pipeline.

Natural Voice Interviewer

Gathers incident description naturally, maintaining full context and session state across websocket messages.

Multimodal Vision Analyst

Inspects uploaded claim photos pixel-by-pixel, using Gemini Vision API to verify authenticity and assess damage severity.

Fraud Detective

Cross-references transcript history, claim logs, and vision assessments to highlight any potential inconsistencies or duplications.
