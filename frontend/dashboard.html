Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit evidence for a claim?

During your conversation with the AI agent, you can click the attachment icon (paperclip) to upload photos. The Vision Agent will automatically analyze them.

What happens after I get a verdict?

If approved, your payout is processed automatically within 24 hours. If flagged, a human agent will review your case. Denied claims can be appealed via email.

Is the voice AI secure?

Yes, all audio is processed in real-time, encrypted, and immediately discarded after transcription. Only the text transcript is stored for compliance.

Still need help?

Our human support team is available 24/7.
